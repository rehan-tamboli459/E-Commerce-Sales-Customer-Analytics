# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, product category revenue, payment methods, and customer reviews using the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset.

## Dashboard Preview

![E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Project Overview

This project transforms e-commerce data into business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, KPI development, and interactive visualizations in Power BI.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze product revenue and order volume.
- Identify the highest-revenue product categories.
- Understand payment method distribution.
- Explore customer review score patterns.
- Examine the geographic distribution of orders across Brazilian states.
- Track monthly revenue trends.

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Product revenue, orders, unique customers, and average product revenue per order.
- **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Revenue performance over time.
- **Top 10 Product Categories:** Product categories ranked by revenue.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Payment value distribution by payment type.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Breakdown of order statuses.
- **Geographic Analysis:** Orders by Brazilian state.
- **Customer Review Analysis:** Review score distribution with an interactive score filter.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — KPI measures and calculated columns
- **Data Modeling** — relationships between orders, customers, products, payments, and reviews
- **CSV** — cleaned data files

## Dataset

The project uses the public Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

Dataset source: [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

## Key Insights

- Product revenue totals approximately R$13.59 million.
- The dashboard contains approximately 99.44K distinct orders.
- Credit cards represent the largest share of payment value.
- Review score 5 is the most frequent score in the current dashboard.
- Revenue varies over time, making monthly trend analysis useful for understanding sales performance.
